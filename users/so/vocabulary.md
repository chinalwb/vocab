<a id="n-thanks-on-the-subway"></a>
## 谢谢你对 App 感兴趣,也谢谢你让我拍下那道题

- 类型:Note
- 日期:2026-10-23
- 掌握:会写

**含义:** 地铁上遇到之后的一段感谢留言:原句,和两个更地道的版本。

**原句:**

> Thanks for your interests on the App and I appreciate your kindness for allowing me to take a photo of that question you showed me on subway. This makes me happy; and hope you have a good day too.

**改了哪些地方:**

- **your interests on** → **your interest in** —— 「对……的兴趣」里 *interest* 不可数,介词用 *in*。
- **your kindness for allowing me** → **you letting me** —— 更口语、更自然;要保留 *kindness* 的话说 *your kindness in letting me*。
- **on subway** → **on the subway** —— 说乘坐的交通工具要加 *the*。
- **This makes me happy** → **That made my day** —— 事情已经发生,用过去时;*made my day* 是「让我开心了一整天」的地道说法。
- **; and hope** → **— I hope** —— 分号后面不接 *and*;*hope* 前面补上主语 *I*。

**更地道的说法:**

1. Thanks for your **interest in** the app, and I really appreciate **you letting me** take a photo of the question you showed me on the subway. That **made my day** — I hope you have a great day too!
   谢谢你对这个 App 感兴趣,也很感谢你让我拍下你在地铁上给我看的那道题。这让我开心了一整天——也祝你今天过得愉快!
2. Thanks so much for your **interest in** the app, and for **letting me** snap a photo of that question on the subway. It **made my day** — have a great one!
   非常感谢你对这个 App 感兴趣,也谢谢你在地铁上让我拍下那道题。真的让我开心了一整天——祝你今天愉快!

<a id="it-could-be-argued-that"></a>
## It could be argued that …

- 词性:phrase
- CEFR:C1
- 类型:写作
- 日期:2026-10-23
- 掌握:会写

**English definition:** A hedged way to introduce a point, so that the writer does not overstate it.

**含义:** 「可以说……;有人可能会说……」。在 Task 2 里引出一个观点,又不把话说死。语气得体(hedging)是写作 7 分以上的特征之一。

**例句**
1. **It could be argued that** working from home improves productivity.
   可以说,在家办公提高了工作效率。
2. **It could be argued that** governments should invest more in public transport than in new roads.
   有人可能会说,政府应该把更多钱投到公共交通上,而不是修新路。

**常见搭配:** *It could be argued that …* / *Some would argue that …* / *It is often argued that …* —— 都用来引出一个观点,后面常接自己的反驳。

**相关条目:** [While it is true that …, …](#while-it-is-true-that) —— 先让步,再给自己的观点

<a id="while-it-is-true-that"></a>
## While it is true that …, …

- 词性:phrase
- CEFR:C1
- 类型:写作
- 日期:2026-10-23
- 掌握:会写

**English definition:** Used to admit that one point is true before making a stronger, opposing point.

**含义:** 「诚然……,但……」。先承认对方说得有道理,再给出自己的观点。让步段是 Task 2「任务回应」拿高分的常见写法。

**例句**
1. **While it is true that** technology saves time, it also makes it harder to switch off from work.
   诚然,科技节省了时间,但它也让人更难从工作中抽身。
2. **While it is true that** tuition fees are high, a degree still pays off for most graduates.
   虽然学费确实很高,但对大多数毕业生来说,学位仍然是值得的。

**易错:** *While* 引导的从句后面不再加 *but*:不说 *While it is true that …, but …*。

**相关条目:** [It could be argued that …](#it-could-be-argued-that) —— 引出观点时留余地

## off the top of my head

- 词性:phrase
- CEFR:C1
- 类型:口语
- 日期:2026-10-23
- 掌握:会写

**English definition:** Without checking or thinking for long; used when giving a quick, approximate answer.

**含义:** 「我随口说一下;一下子想到的是」。Part 3 遇到没准备过的问题时,用它开头很自然,也给自己争取一点思考时间。

**例句**
1. **Off the top of my head**, I'd say about half of my friends live abroad.
   我随口估计一下,大概一半的朋友住在国外。
2. I can't remember the exact name **off the top of my head**, but it was a small café near the station.
   我一下子想不起确切的名字,不过是车站附近的一家小咖啡馆。

<a id="a-double-edged-sword"></a>
## a double-edged sword

- 词性:phrase
- CEFR:C1
- 类型:口语
- 日期:2026-10-23
- 掌握:会写

**English definition:** Something that has both good and bad effects.

**含义:** 「一把双刃剑」,有利也有弊。Part 3 谈科技、社交媒体、全球化这类话题时很好用。

**例句**
1. Social media is **a double-edged sword**: it keeps us connected, but it can also be addictive.
   社交媒体是一把双刃剑:它让我们保持联系,但也可能让人上瘾。
2. Working from home is **a bit of a double-edged sword** for me.
   在家办公对我来说有点像一把双刃剑。

**提示:** 说完 *a double-edged sword*,紧接着给出好的一面和坏的一面,回答才完整。

<a id="pose-a-threat-to"></a>
## pose a threat to

- 词性:phrase
- CEFR:C1
- 类型:高分搭配
- 日期:2026-10-23
- 掌握:会写

**English definition:** To be a danger to someone or something.

**含义:** 「对……构成威胁」,比 *is dangerous to* 更书面,适合 Task 2。

**例句**
1. Rising sea levels **pose a serious threat to** coastal cities.
   海平面上升对沿海城市构成严重威胁。
2. Some experts believe that AI could **pose a threat to** certain jobs.
   一些专家认为,人工智能可能会对某些工作构成威胁。

**易错:** 动词用 *pose*,不说 *make a threat to* 或 *bring a threat to*。

<a id="bear-the-brunt-of"></a>
## bear the brunt of

- 词性:phrase
- CEFR:C2
- 类型:高分搭配
- 日期:2026-10-23
- 掌握:会写

**English definition:** To suffer the worst part of something unpleasant.

**含义:** 「首当其冲;承受最大的冲击」。写经济、环境、政策的影响时常用。

**例句**
1. Low-income families often **bear the brunt of** rising food prices.
   低收入家庭往往首当其冲,受食品涨价的冲击最大。
2. Small businesses **bore the brunt of** the economic downturn.
   小企业在经济低迷中受到的冲击最大。

**提示:** 过去式是 *bore*,过去分词是 *borne*。

<a id="mitigate"></a>
## mitigate

- 音标:/ˈmɪtɪɡeɪt/
- 词性:v.
- CEFR:C1
- 类型:学术词汇
- 日期:2026-10-23
- 掌握:会写

**English definition:** To make something bad less severe or less harmful.

**含义:** 减轻、缓解(坏的影响)。Task 2 写对策时常用,比 *reduce* 更学术。

**例句**
1. Planting more trees can help **mitigate the effects of** air pollution.
   多种树有助于减轻空气污染的影响。
2. The government introduced new measures to **mitigate the impact of** rising rents.
   政府出台了新措施,缓解房租上涨带来的影响。

**常见搭配:** *mitigate the effects / impact / risk of …*

**相关条目:** [exacerbate](#exacerbate) —— 意思相反:使恶化

<a id="exacerbate"></a>
## exacerbate

- 音标:英 /ɪɡˈzæsəbeɪt/ 美 /ɪɡˈzæsərbeɪt/
- 词性:v.
- CEFR:C1
- 类型:学术词汇
- 日期:2026-10-23
- 掌握:会写

**English definition:** To make a problem, a bad situation or a negative feeling worse.

**含义:** 使(问题)恶化、加剧。常和 *mitigate* 出现在同一篇作文里:一边说什么让问题更糟,一边说怎么缓解。

**例句**
1. Building more roads may actually **exacerbate** traffic congestion.
   多修路反而可能加剧交通拥堵。
2. Long working hours can **exacerbate** stress and health problems.
   长时间工作会加重压力和健康问题。

**相关条目:** [mitigate](#mitigate) —— 意思相反:减轻
