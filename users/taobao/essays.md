## 地震来了,政府先做什么

- 日期:2026-10-31

**题目:**

课本上的一道「说理由」练习:如果你是一个常有地震的国家的政府官员,你会优先做哪件事、把最多的钱和人力放在哪里?题目给了四个选择:加固旧楼、保证新楼能防震、教大家地震安全知识、研究预测地震的方法。

详细的批改分析:[地震作文批改](https://claude.ai/artifact/NmRFSsBe1wnGr9j9bmnA2S)

**改好的文章:**

第 1 段

1. I don't think **reinforcing** old buildings is **a realistic plan**, as it costs too much and it will **put extra pressure on** the government.
   我觉得加固旧楼不是一个现实的计划,因为太花钱,还会给政府带来额外的压力。
2. The plan which we need to prioritize is **to research** ways to predict earthquakes.
   我们需要优先做的计划,是研究预测地震的方法。
3. In that way, we can evacuate people from **dangerous areas before an earthquake hits**.
   这样,我们就能在地震来之前,把人从危险的地方撤走。

第 2 段

1. Also, **making** new buildings earthquake-proof is important.
   另外,让新楼能防震也很重要。
2. We can achieve this by adding bolted foundations, **shock absorbers**, and **steel frames**.
   我们可以加上用螺栓固定的地基、减震器和钢结构来做到这一点。
3. If **new buildings** are less likely to collapse, people can just **stay safe** in **their houses**.
   如果新楼不那么容易倒塌,大家待在自己家里就很安全。

第 3 段

1. If we combine **these** two ways, **with prediction as our top priority**, earthquakes will cause less destruction and loss of life.
   如果我们把这两个办法结合起来,并且把预测放在第一位,地震造成的破坏和伤亡就会更少。

加粗的是改过的地方。

**原文:**

> I don't think reinforce old buildings is a plan that can be perfect in reality as it costs too much and it will bring new stress to the government. The plan which we need to prioritize is research ways to predict earthquakes. In that way, we can evacuate people from the dangerous region and move them to a safer place.
>
> Also, make new buildings earthquake-proof is important. We can achieve this goal by adding bolted foundations, Shock Absorbers, and Steel Skeletons. If all the structures are less likely to collapse, people can just stay in their house.
>
> If we can combine those two ways together, earthquakes will cause less destruction and loss of life.

**这篇的条目:**

- [动作当主语,用 -ing:Reinforcing old buildings is…](#s-ing-subject) —— reinforce → **reinforcing**,make → **making**
- [计划是去做什么:The plan is to research…](#s-plan-is-to) —— is research → **is to research**
- [给……压力:put pressure on someone](#s-put-pressure-on) —— bring new stress to → **put extra pressure on**
- [现实中行得通:a realistic plan](#s-realistic-plan) —— a plan that can be perfect in reality → **a realistic plan**
- [很多人,很多个家:their houses](#s-their-houses) —— their house → **their houses**
- [stay safe:stay + 形容词](#c-stay-safe) —— 新加的 **stay safe**
